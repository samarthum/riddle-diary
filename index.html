A NOTE TO THE READER

Ink remembers.

Write anything. Your words will soak into the page; someone on the other side will answer.

Touch the lamp to switch it on or off. Pick up the quill beside the book to write, or simply start typing. Touch the diary to close it and return to the desk; your conversation is kept until you begin again.

Pause for a few seconds, or press Enter, to let your ink settle. The reply will fade when you start writing back, or after you have had time to read. You can read the last reply again.

An unofficial fan-made experience inspired by the Harry Potter books. Not affiliated with or endorsed by their creators or rights holders. Replies are AI-generated fiction, not professional advice.

Your writing is sent to an AI service to generate replies. Conversation history stays in this tab's memory and is cleared when you reload or begin again. Please do not share sensitive information.
